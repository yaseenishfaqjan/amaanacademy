Online Quran Learning for American Muslim Families

Living in the United States as a Muslim family comes with unique challenges when it comes to Islamic education. Many American Muslims live in areas without local madrasas or qualified Quran teachers. Amaan Academy bridges this gap by providing one-on-one online Quran classes specifically tailored for families across the USA, including families in New York, Chicago, Los Angeles, Houston, and Dallas.

Why American Families Choose Amaan Academy

  • US Timezone Friendly — Classes scheduled for Eastern, Central, Mountain, and Pacific time zones
  • English-Speaking Teachers — Clear communication in English for children growing up in America
  • Urdu Available — Classes can also be conducted in Urdu for families who prefer their native language
  • No Mosque Required — Learn from the comfort and safety of your home
  • One-on-One Attention — Unlike crowded weekend schools, your child gets full teacher attention
  • Free Trial — Try a class with no commitment before enrolling
