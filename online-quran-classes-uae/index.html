Quran Learning for Families Across the UAE

From Dubai and Abu Dhabi to Sharjah and the wider Emirates, Amaan Academy brings a qualified, certified Aalim directly into your home over video call. Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer online Quran classes across the UAE?

Yes. We teach Muslim families throughout the United Arab Emirates — Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and beyond — one-on-one with a certified Aalim, scheduled in Gulf Standard Time (GST).

Can my child learn in English or Urdu in the UAE?

Absolutely. Classes are available in both English and Urdu, which suits the UAE's diverse expat and local families. You can also request a male or female teacher.

How do online Quran classes work for UAE families?

Lessons run live over Zoom or Google Meet, one-on-one, at times that fit your day in the UAE. All you need is a device and internet, and your first 30-minute trial class is completely free.
