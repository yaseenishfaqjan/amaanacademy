Frequently Asked Questions

Which Quran program should I start with?

If you or your child cannot yet read Arabic, start with Nazra & Qaida. Once reading is fluent, most students move on to Tajweed, and then to Hifz if they wish to memorise. Your teacher assesses the right starting point during the free trial.

Can I switch programs later?

Yes. Learning the Quran is a journey. Many students begin with Qaida, progress to Tajweed, and later start Hifz or the Aalimiyat course. Your one-on-one teacher adjusts the plan as you grow.

Are all programs one-on-one?

Yes. Every Amaan Academy program is taught one-on-one with a certified Aalim or Aalima, so the pace, language (English or Urdu) and schedule are fully tailored to the student.
