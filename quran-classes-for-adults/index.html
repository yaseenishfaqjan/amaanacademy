Frequently Asked Questions

Is it too late to learn the Quran as an adult?

It is never too late. Many of our adult students start as complete beginners and learn to read the Quran fluently. Because classes are one-on-one, there is no embarrassment and no fixed pace — you simply progress from wherever you are today.

Can I learn around a full-time job?

Yes. We schedule adult classes early morning, late evening, or on weekends to fit around work in your timezone. Sessions are typically 30–60 minutes, 2–5 times a week, and are easy to reschedule.

Do you have female teachers for adult sisters?

Yes. Qualified female Aalimas teach adult sisters who prefer a female Quran teacher, in a private and comfortable one-on-one setting.
