About Our Lead Teacher

Meet Abdul Rahman — Hafiz ul Quran & Aalim

Abdul Rahman is the founder and lead teacher at Amaan Academy. He is a Hafiz ul Quran with a Master's degree in Islamic Studies from Dawat-e-Islami, where he achieved a remarkable score of 500 out of 487 marks. He also holds an Aalimiya degree from the prestigious Kanz-ul-Madaris with a score of 800 out of 773.

With over 13 years of teaching experience, he has served as an Imam for 7 years and as a Khateeb for 6 years in addition to his online teaching work. His teaching approach combines traditional Islamic scholarship with modern online education methods to deliver effective one-on-one Quran classes.

We teach families across the world. He is also supported by qualified female Aalima teachers including Amna Tahir, a Senior Aalima with over 10 years of experience, for sisters and young female students.

Our Female Teacher

Learn from Amna Tahir — Senior Aalima

Amna Tahir is our senior female Aalima with over 10 years of experience teaching Quran and Islamic studies to women and young girls. She holds formal qualifications in Quranic studies, Tajweed, and Islamic education from recognized institutions.

She specializes in teaching Nazra & Qaida, Tajweed, and basic Islamic studies to young female students and sisters who prefer learning from a qualified female teacher. Her gentle, patient approach makes students feel comfortable and motivated to learn.

At Amaan Academy, we understand that many families prefer their daughters to learn from a female teacher. Amna Tahir provides that option, ensuring every girl receives quality Quranic education in a comfortable and appropriate learning environment.
