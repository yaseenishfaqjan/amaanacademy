Frequently Asked Questions

What is an Ijazah?

An Ijazah is a formal certification, granted by a qualified scholar, that authorises the holder to teach and transmit the Quran (and Islamic sciences) to others. It is only given after the student has demonstrated mastery of recitation and memorisation to the standard of their teacher.

What is a Sanad (chain of transmission)?

A Sanad is the unbroken chain of teachers through which the Quran has been passed down, teacher to student, generation after generation, going back to the Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم. It is the traditional guarantee that what is taught today matches exactly what was revealed.

Why does this matter when choosing an online academy?

Many websites advertise anonymous “certified tutors.” Learning from a teacher with a documented Ijazah and Sanad means you are learning authentic recitation from a verified, authorised source — not a self-appointed one. It is the strongest assurance of quality in Quranic teaching.
