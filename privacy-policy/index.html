Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

Amaan Academy ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website and use our online Quran learning services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, WhatsApp number, country, and your child's name and age when you fill out our booking form.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you access and use our website, including your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on pages.
  • Communication Data: Records of our correspondence with you via WhatsApp, email, or other channels.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Schedule and conduct your online Quran classes
  • Communicate with you about your classes, scheduling, and progress
  • Send you monthly progress reports about your child's learning
  • Improve our website and teaching services
  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support

3. How We Protect Your Information

We take appropriate security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Your information is stored securely and only shared with your assigned teacher for the purpose of conducting your classes. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

4. WhatsApp Communication

By submitting our booking form, you consent to being contacted via WhatsApp regarding your free trial class and ongoing Quran lessons. You can opt out of WhatsApp communications at any time by messaging us to unsubscribe.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as you are an active student, and for a reasonable period afterward to handle any follow-up inquiries or re-enrollments. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of communications at any time

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us at info@amaanacademy.com or via WhatsApp at +92 328 2347875.
