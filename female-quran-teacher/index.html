Meet Amna Tahir — Senior Aalima

Amna Tahir is a Senior Aalima with more than 10 years of experience teaching the Quran to women and girls. She specialises in Nazra, Qaida and Tajweed, and guides students gently from their very first Arabic letters through to fluent, beautiful recitation.

Her calm, encouraging teaching style helps shy beginners and young children feel at ease, while her strong grounding in Islamic studies supports older students who want to deepen their knowledge.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you have a female Quran teacher?

Yes. Amna Tahir is a Senior Aalima with over 10 years of experience teaching Nazra, Qaida, Tajweed and Islamic studies to women and young girls. She teaches in a warm, patient and modest learning environment.

Can my daughter learn Quran with a female teacher online?

Absolutely. Many families prefer a female teacher for their daughters. Our female Aalima teaches girls of all ages one-on-one, with parents welcome to observe, in English or Urdu.

Are the classes suitable for adult women?

Yes. Adult sisters — including complete beginners and reverts — can learn Qaida, Nazra, Tajweed and more with our female teacher at times that suit their schedule and timezone.

Is there a free trial with the female teacher?

Yes. You can book a free 30-minute trial class with our female Aalima on WhatsApp, with no obligation.
