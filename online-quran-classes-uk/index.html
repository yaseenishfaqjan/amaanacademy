Quranic Education for British Muslim Families

For Muslim families living in the United Kingdom, finding quality Quranic education can be challenging. While many cities have mosques with madrasas, the crowded weekend school format often fails to provide the individual attention children need. Amaan Academy offers a better solution — one-on-one online Quran classes with a certified Aalim, delivered directly to your home anywhere in the UK, including London, Manchester, Birmingham, and Leeds.
