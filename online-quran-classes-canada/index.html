Quran Classes for Canadian Muslim Families

Canada is home to a growing and vibrant Muslim community spread across vast distances — from the bustling cities of Toronto, Vancouver, Calgary, Montreal, and Ottawa to smaller communities in Alberta, Saskatchewan, and the Atlantic provinces. Many Canadian Muslim families struggle to find qualified Quran teachers, especially outside major urban centers. Amaan Academy solves this problem by bringing one-on-one online Quran classes with a certified Aalim to every Canadian home, regardless of location.

  • Certified Teacher — Learn from a qualified Aalim with authentic credentials
