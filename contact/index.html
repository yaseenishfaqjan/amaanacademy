Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will you reply?

We usually reply on WhatsApp within a few hours during the day. Because our teachers and coordinators serve families across many timezones, you can message us any time and we will get back to you as soon as possible.

What information should I include?

Let us know who the class is for (an adult or a child, and their age), which program you are interested in, whether you prefer a male or female teacher, and a few times that suit your schedule. This helps us arrange the best free trial for you.

Is contacting you any obligation?

Not at all. Reaching out and booking a free trial class carries no cost and no obligation. You only decide on a plan after experiencing the teaching for yourself.
